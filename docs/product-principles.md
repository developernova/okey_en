# Product principles

Catch English exists to help a person understand and use real English, not to
maximize time in the product.

## The promise

The product should provide a complete and genuinely useful free learning
experience. Monetization, if it appears later, must not make the core learning
cycle deliberately weaker, slower, or frustrating.

## What we optimize for

- Real comprehension of English in video and conversation.
- Useful phrases noticed in context, not isolated vocabulary lists.
- A short daily plan with a clear end, so the user can learn and leave.
- Evidence of language that was remembered or used independently. Watching,
  opening, or saving content is activity, not progress.
- Calm, respectful guidance without pressure, guilt, or artificial urgency.

## What we do not optimize for

- Retention for retention's sake, session length, or compulsive reopening.
- Streak anxiety, infinite feeds, loot-box mechanics, or manipulative reminders.
- Paywalls that block the complete core learning cycle.
- Inflated counters and celebrations that substitute for learning.
- Repeating words a learner has already demonstrated they know.

## Product areas are intentionally separate

- **Today** is the complete core learning cycle. It is self-contained and does
  not pull tasks from entertainment or optional practice areas.
- **My phrases** is a calm view of phrases that are difficult or being
  forgotten. Optional conversation practice may reinforce a phrase, but it
  must not grade the learner or change the spaced-repetition schedule.
- **Watch** is an optional catalog of films, series, and other English content.
  Choosing, watching, explaining, or saving a moment there must not change the
  daily plan or the spaced-repetition schedule.

Only a scheduled review completed inside the core learning cycle may update
spaced-repetition state. Extra exposure, replay, explanation, and conversation
practice can be recorded as neutral history, never as a review result.

## Watching is not the method

The catalog may confirm that learning works and make contact with English more
pleasant, but it must not impersonate the core learning cycle. It is a
thermometer, not the source of heat.

- No infinite feed, autoplay chain, engagement ranking, streak, or reward for
  continued watching.
- The learner chooses a title or finite collection before playback.
- Every viewing sequence has a visible end. Continuing requires an explicit
  choice.
- Watch time, scene count, and scrolling never become learning progress.
- The product may help explain any moment, but it does not turn every lookup
  into a card or every viewed phrase into scheduled repetition.

## Product test

Before adding a feature, ask:

1. Does this help the learner understand, remember, or say something useful?
2. Does it respect the learner's time and attention?
3. Would we still build it if retention were not a success metric?
4. Can the learner finish today's useful work and leave without penalty?

If the answer is no, the feature does not belong in Catch English.
